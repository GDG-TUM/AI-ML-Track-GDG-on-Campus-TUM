# Week 11: Demo day

**When:** Phase 2 (draft), week 11. Time and venue are posted in the track channel.

**Goal:** Show what you built, learn from each other, and plan the next phase.

## Learning objectives

By the end you should be able to:

- Present a project clearly to a mixed audience in five minutes
- Give and receive specific, kind feedback
- Reflect on what worked, what did not and what you would change
- Decide how you want to keep contributing

## Before the session

- [ ] Project README, model card, data card and checklist are merged
- [ ] Demo works, and a **backup recording** exists
- [ ] Rehearse twice, with a timer. **5 minutes of demo plus 3 minutes of questions.**
- [ ] Optional: at most **three slides**. A working demo beats slides.

## Agenda

1. Welcome and how the day works (5 min)
2. **Project demos:** 5 minutes of demo and 3 minutes of questions per team (about 50 min for six teams)
3. Mentor and maintainer feedback (10 min)
4. **Retrospective:** what worked, what to change, what to keep (15 min)
5. Recognition, thanks and what comes next (10 min)

## The five-minute demo

| Minute | What |
|:-:|---|
| 0:00 | **The problem:** who has it, and why it matters |
| 0:45 | **The solution:** what you built, in one sentence |
| 1:15 | **Live demo:** show it working, including one failure and what you learned from it |
| 3:30 | **How you know it works:** baseline, metric and honest evaluation |
| 4:15 | **Responsible AI:** who could be harmed, and what you did about it |
| 4:45 | **What is next** and a thank-you |

## Feedback guide

Mentors and maintainers give feedback with these five questions in mind. The scores are for **conversation**, not a ranking. Every team that finishes is recognised.

| Question | What good looks like |
|---|---|
| **Problem and impact** | A real need, a clear audience |
| **Technical soundness** | A sensible approach, working code, a reproducible result |
| **Honest evaluation** | A baseline, the right metric, no leakage, mistakes examined |
| **Responsible AI** | Risks named, slices tested, limits documented |
| **Communication and demo** | Clear story, a working demo, good use of the time |

## 🛡️ Responsible AI moment

Share **one thing you would now do differently** in a project because of what you learned about fairness, privacy or safety.
