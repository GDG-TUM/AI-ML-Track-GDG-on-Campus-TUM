# Week 4: Neural networks from scratch

**Date:** Wed 4 Nov (draft; confirm time and venue in the track channel)

**Goal:** Remove the magic from deep learning by training a neural network with nothing but NumPy, then pitch a project idea.

## Learning objectives

By the end you should be able to:

- Explain weights, biases, activation functions, loss and gradients in plain words
- Describe backpropagation as "the chain rule, applied backwards"
- Use a **gradient check** to verify that your maths is right
- See what the learning rate, network size and activation do to training
- Pitch a project idea in 60 seconds

## Before the session

- [ ] Finish [Week 3](../week-03-evaluate-honestly/README.md)
- [ ] Optional but great: watch the first video of [3Blue1Brown's neural network series](https://www.3blue1brown.com/topics/neural-networks)
- [ ] Think of **one problem** that matters to you or your community. You will pitch it.

## Agenda

1. Arrive and set up (10 min)
2. Goal and recap (5 min)
3. Concept: from a line to a network, loss, gradient, backprop (15 min)
4. Notebook in pairs: build, check and train a network (35 min)
5. **Project pitches:** 60 seconds each, volunteers first (15 min)
6. 🛡️ Responsible AI moment (5 min)
7. Wrap-up and take-home challenge (5 min)

### The pitch (60 seconds)

1. **The problem:** who has it?
2. **The idea:** what would the model or app do?
3. **The data:** where would it come from?
4. **The ask:** who wants to join?

Pitches are open to everyone, including beginners. See the [project guide](../../projects/README.md) and [ideas](../../projects/ideas.md).

## 🛡️ Responsible AI moment

For your pitch idea: **what data would it need, and who are the people in that data?** Would they be happy to know how it is used?

## 🏁 Take-home challenge

- [ ] Try the five experiments at the bottom of the notebook. **Predict first, then run.**
- [ ] Write your pitch as five lines and post it in the track channel to find teammates
- [ ] Form a team of **3 to 5** (mixed experience is a strength)
- [ ] Start your **project proposal** (the form is in the [project guide](../../projects/README.md)). It is due at the end of Week 5.
