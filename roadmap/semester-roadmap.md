## Phases

1. **Foundations (weeks 1 to 2):** data, notebooks, a first model
2. **Core ML and deep learning (weeks 3 to 5):** honest evaluation, neural networks, transfer learning. Projects form.
3. **Generative AI (weeks 6 to 7):** language models, RAG, agents
4. **Ship it responsibly (weeks 8 to 9):** the responsible AI review, tests and a demo
5. **Show (week 10):** demo day and retrospective

## Principles

- **Learn by doing:** every session ends with something working
- **Small steps:** challenges take 1 to 2 hours
- **Honest first:** baselines, validation and real mistakes before bragging
- **Responsible by default:** a five-minute Responsible AI moment every week
- **Free and browser-first:** no GPU, no paid tools
- **Public by default:** work goes on GitHub
- **Help each other:** teach what you just learned
