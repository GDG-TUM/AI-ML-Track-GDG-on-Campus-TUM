# 0001. We record decisions

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-10-05
- **Deciders:** Track Lead

## Context

The track's leadership changes every academic year, as students graduate or move on. Knowledge that lives only in people's heads is lost at each handover. New leads then either repeat old mistakes or undo good choices without knowing why they were made.

## Decision

We will keep short, numbered **decision records** in `docs/decisions/`, based on the [template](0000-template.md), for any decision that is hard to reverse, affects many people, or that someone may later question.

## Alternatives considered

- **Meeting notes or chat history:** searchable only by people who remember where to look, and they disappear when channels are archived.
- **A wiki:** drifts out of date and sits outside code review.
- **Long design documents:** too heavy for a student volunteer community.

## Consequences

- **Good:** continuity across years, and a place for disagreement to end with a written reason.
- **Bad:** a small amount of writing, so we keep records to one page.
- **Revisit when:** nobody has written a record for a full semester. That means the habit has died.
