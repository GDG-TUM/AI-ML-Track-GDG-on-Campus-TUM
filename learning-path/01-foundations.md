# Stage 1: Foundations

**Goal:** Be able to look at a dataset, ask questions of it and plot it, in Python, in your browser.

**Matching session:** [Week 1: Welcome and Python for data](../weekly-sessions/week-01-welcome-and-python-for-data/README.md)

## What you will learn

- What machine learning is: finding patterns in examples instead of writing rules by hand
- How AI, machine learning, deep learning and generative AI relate to each other
- Working in notebooks, on Colab or Kaggle
- **NumPy** (arrays), **pandas** (tables) and **matplotlib** (charts)
- The few maths ideas you will use every day: average, spread, correlation, and the idea of a slope

## Hands-on

1. Open the [Week 1 notebook](../weekly-sessions/week-01-welcome-and-python-for-data/python_for_data.ipynb) in Colab.
2. Finish the take-home challenge, then explore a dataset of your own choosing from [Kaggle Datasets](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets).
3. Add your profile to the repo. Your first pull request.

## New to programming?

That is fine. Spend a few hours on these first, and tell the Track Lead so we can pair you with a mentor:

- [Kaggle Learn: Python](https://www.kaggle.com/learn/python), short interactive lessons
- [Kaggle Learn: Pandas](https://www.kaggle.com/learn/pandas)

## ✅ Checkpoint

- Can I explain machine learning to a friend in two sentences, without jargon?
- Can I load a table, summarise it and make a chart?
- What does a correlation of 0.5 tell me, and what does it *not* tell me?
- Can I run a notebook top to bottom and save my own copy?

If you can answer these, move to the next stage. If not, revisit the topics or ask in the track channel.
