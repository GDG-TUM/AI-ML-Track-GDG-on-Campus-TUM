# Stage 5: Responsible AI

**Goal:** Build things that are useful **and** fair, private, safe and honest, and be able to show it.

**Matching session:** [Week 8: Responsible AI](../weekly-sessions/week-08-responsible-ai/README.md), plus a five-minute moment in **every** session.

## What you will learn

- Where harm comes from: data, labels, models, deployment and misuse
- **Fairness**: evaluating on slices and reading gaps honestly
- **Privacy and consent**, including Kenya's Data Protection Act, 2019
- **Transparency**: writing a **model card** and a **data card**
- **Safety**: failure modes, human oversight and prompt injection
- **Red-teaming** your own project
- When *not* to build something

## Hands-on

1. Write a [model card](../projects/_template/MODEL_CARD.md) and [data card](../projects/_template/DATA_CARD.md) for your Week 3 model or your project.
2. Evaluate your model on at least two **slices** of your data and report any gap.
3. Complete the [responsible AI checklist](../resources/responsible-ai-checklist.md), then red-team another team's project.

Read the full working agreement in the [Responsible AI guide](../docs/responsible-ai.md).

## ✅ Checkpoint

- Who could be harmed if my model is wrong, and how badly?
- My model has 92% accuracy overall. What would I check next?
- What belongs in a model card, and who reads it?
- Why is "we removed the sensitive column" not enough to make a model fair?
- What would make me stop a project?
